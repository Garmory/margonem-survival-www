First launch

  1. macOS: unzip, then right-click the app → Open (the test build is unsigned).
  2. Windows: unzip the whole folder and run MargonemSurvival.exe. If SmartScreen asks: More info → Run anyway.

Signing in

The game signs you in with your Google account — click SIGN IN WITH GOOGLE, finish in the browser and return to the game. During the closed test only invited accounts can enter; next launches sign in on their own.

Test world

Everyone plays on a shared test world in the cloud. It may be wiped without warning — characters and camps can vanish, and bug reports are worth their weight in gold. When the game asks for an update, grab a fresh build from this page.
